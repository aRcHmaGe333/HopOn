The Vision: Zero-Detour Urban Micro-Mobility

HopOn turns everyday commuter traffic into an instant, distributed transit mesh. Walking passengers are matched in real-time with private drivers who are already traveling along their exact corridor.

  • Zero Driver Detour: Passengers are accepted only when their destination lies along the driver’s declared route (< 50m tolerance).
  • Safe Walk-to-Ride Pickup: System routes passengers to safe boarding zones (crosswalks, side streets) avoiding active traffic lanes and geofenced hazards.
  • Strict 30-Second Boarding: Drivers never wait > 30 seconds at the curb; automated timeout and reassignment protect driver efficiency.
  • Synchronized Color Handshake: Matching visual color codes on passenger and driver screens enable instant identification in busy streets.
  • 50–70% Lower Fares: Predictable, transparent pricing that offsets commuter fuel costs while maintaining a lean 10–15% platform take.
