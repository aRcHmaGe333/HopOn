HopOn

Step out. Walk a little. Get picked up immediately.

Elevator pitch

HopOn connects passengers and drivers whose routes already align, enabling immediate pickups with minimal detours, improved sustainability, and affordable fares.

  • Route alignment: Assign rides only when passenger destination lies along the driver’s route.
  • Safety-first pickups: Recommend safe boarding points; avoid busy lanes.
  • Fast handoff: Visual color-code handshake; drivers wait <30s.
  • Low fares: Simple, predictable pricing and a small platform cut.

Design and MVP scaffold are in place. The repo contains the DoD, backlog, and design notes for stakeholders and contributors.

How it works — high level

  1. Driver declares route; passenger requests a ride with destination.
  2. Matching engine checks route overlap and recommends safe pickup points.
  3. Apps show ETA and a color-code handshake for identification.
  4. Driver waits <30s; deviations are recorded and enforced.
