## 3. Application Copy Bank (Ready-to-Submit)

### One-Liner / Tagline (Under 10 words)
> Instant walk-to-ride pickup connecting walking passengers with matching commuter cars.

### 50-Word Elevator Summary
> HopOn is an instant walk-to-ride platform that matches walking pedestrians with private drivers whose routes already align with their destination. With zero driver detours, safe walk-to-curb boarding zones, and a strict 30-second dwell time, HopOn provides rides at 50% of taxi fares while offsetting fuel costs for everyday commuters.

### 100-Word Comprehensive Description
> HopOn transforms urban mobility by converting everyday commuter traffic into a real-time transit mesh. Rather than dispatching dedicated cars that cause deadhead miles and traffic congestion, HopOn's deterministic matching engine pairs walking passengers exclusively with drivers already passing their destination. Passengers walk to safe, pre-calculated curb boarding points and verify drivers instantly via a synchronized color-code screen handshake. Drivers never detour and never wait more than 30 seconds. The result is affordable micro-transit (50–70% cheaper than traditional cabs), reduced urban congestion, and an asset-light, sustainable alternative to municipal bus route expansion.

### What is your unfair advantage / defensibility?
> Unlike traditional ride-hailing networks burdened with heavy driver acquisition costs, deadhead miles, and regulatory resistance, HopOn is an asset-light, corridor-focused software protocol. Our deterministic route-overlap algorithm prevents route detours, enabling frictionless micro-carpooling with zero schedule friction. Our intellectual property, route matching heuristics, and protocol architecture are published and cryptographically anchored under the IPClaim APC license (RFC 3161 timestamped), establishing incontrovertible prior art and author provenance.

### Why Now?
1. **Cost of Living & Fuel Inflation**: Commuters urgently need ways to subsidize their daily car ownership costs without becoming full-time gig workers.
2. **Congestion Pricing & Low-Emission Zones**: Cities are penalizing low-occupancy vehicles; HopOn turns solo commuters into multi-occupant carpools instantly.
3. **Smartphone & GPS Precision**: Modern high-accuracy RTK and pedestrian positioning make sub-50-meter corridor matching and safe curb-side boarding seamless.
